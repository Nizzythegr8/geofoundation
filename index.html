    Engineering Notice: This prototype provides preliminary decision-support analysis based on user-entered parameters. It is not a substitute for a complete geotechnical investigation, detailed foundation design, engineering judgement, or review by a suitably qualified professional engineer. Eurocode 7 results use simplified Annex D factors and Design Approach 2 assumptions; National Annex values and full combination checks should be applied in final design. Groundwater effects use a simplified submerged unit weight approach.
